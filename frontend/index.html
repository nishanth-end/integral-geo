Data Sources & Audit Methodology

Source Documents

All records are extracted directly from official NHAI monthly project reports and MIS monitoring tables published by the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways (MoRTH). Data is parsed verbatim without manual alteration.

Geocoding & Accuracy Scope

Alignments are linearly interpolated along OpenStreetMap highway geometries using official physical milestone chainages (Km start to Km end). Unplaced projects are explicitly documented with reasons rather than omitted.

Temporal Scope

This dataset reflects a single monthly reporting snapshot. It represents active and awarded contracts in the state at the time of report publication, not an exhaustive historical record.
